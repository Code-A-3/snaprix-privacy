# Snaprix — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 3, 2026**

Snaprix is a game developed and maintained by an independent developer.

We respect your privacy and limit the information collected and stored by Snaprix to what is necessary for basic game functionality and player progress.

## 1. Data We Collect and Store

Snaprix collects and stores **only the following four pieces of information**:

- **Player nickname:** A player-selected display name. Nicknames are not required to be unique and do not necessarily identify an individual.
- **Maximum unlocked seed:** The highest board seed the player has unlocked.
- **Total score:** The player's accumulated gameplay score.
- **Unique device ID:** An identifier used to associate game records with a particular device.

**Snaprix does not independently collect or store any other personal information, gameplay records, or device information.**

We do not sell or share these records with advertisers or data brokers.

## 2. Third-Party Services

Snaprix integrates third-party services that operate under their own privacy policies and applicable data protection requirements.

**Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (UMP)**

Google provides advertising and advertising consent management. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information, including device identifiers, IP addresses, and advertising-related data, in accordance with Google's privacy policies and applicable regulations.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Epic Online Services (EOS)**

Epic Online Services provides online authentication, multiplayer connectivity, and related online functionality. Information processed through these services is handled according to Epic Games' privacy policy.

- Epic Games Privacy Policy: https://www.epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy

Snaprix does not independently collect or store additional information processed by these third-party services.

## 3. Advertising Consent

Where required by applicable regulations, Snaprix uses Google's User Messaging Platform to obtain and manage advertising consent.

Players may review or update their advertising privacy preferences through the options made available in the Game.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

Snaprix retains the four data fields described in Section 1 for player identification and progress tracking.

Players may request deletion of information controlled by Snaprix by contacting the developer.

Data collected or processed independently by Google or Epic Games is subject to their respective privacy policies, retention practices, and privacy controls.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game's functionality, data practices, or applicable legal requirements change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
